Introduction
These guidelines contain practical advice for match officials which supplements the information in the Laws of the Game section.
Reference is made in Law 5 to referees operating within the framework of the Laws of the Game and the ‘spirit of the game’. Referees are expected to use common sense and to apply the ‘spirit of the game’ when applying the Laws of the Game, especially when making decisions relating to whether a match takes place and/or continues.
This is especially true for the lower levels of football where it may not always be possible for the Law to be strictly applied. For example, unless there are safety issues, the referee should allow a game to start/continue if:
one or more corner flags are missing
there is a minor inaccuracy with the markings on the field of play such as the corner area, centre circle etc.
the goalposts/crossbar are not white
In such cases, the referee should, with the agreement of the teams, play/continue the match and must submit a report to the appropriate authorities.

FAQs
The referee enters the field of play for the pre-match inspection and sees that one corner flag is missing or the goalposts/crossbar are grey (not white). What should the referee do in this situation?
The Practical Guidelines recommend that the referee:
allows the match to take place (with the agreement of the teams)
submits a report to the appropriate authorities
This is especially important for the lower levels of football where it is not always possible for Law 1 to be strictly applied.
